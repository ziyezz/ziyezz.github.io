Chocolate Cake

This delicious chocolate cake is soft, flufffy, and rich in flavor cake. It takes about 45 minutes to make and is able to feed 24, perfect for a small party. Have fun baking!!

Equipment

  • 2 Cake Pans(9in")
  • A Mixing Bowl
  • A Whisk

      • Ingrediants

      • 2 cups All-Purpose Flour
      • 2 cups Sugar
